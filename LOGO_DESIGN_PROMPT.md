## 2. Designer Brief (Full Spec)

### 2.1 Logo Concept

- **Primary mark:** A heart that feels **modern, premium, and intentional** — not cute or playful. It should work for a serious matrimonial product.
- **Current reference:** Heart with smooth bezier curves; gradient fill pink → rose; thin white stroke; smaller inner heart or highlight in white (low opacity) for depth. You can refine or reimagine this.
- **Optional:** Explore “pairing” ideas (two elements becoming one, two hearts, linked rings) while keeping the mark simple and scalable.

### 2.2 Color Specification

- **Gradient (primary):**  
  - Start: `#ec4899` (pink)  
  - End: `#f43f5e` (rose)  
  - Direction: diagonal (e.g. top-left to bottom-right, 135°).
- **Stroke (if used):** White `#ffffff`, 1–2px (scale with size).
- **Highlight / inner shape:** White at 20–30% opacity.
- **Wordmark:**  
  - Either: “Pair” dark gray `#111827`, “Perfectly” in same gradient.  
  - Or: full swipeupright” in gradient.
- **Tagline:** Gray `#6b7280`, italic.

### 2.3 Typography (Wordmark)

- **Preferred:** Serif for swipeupright” (e.g. Playfair Display) — elegant, marriage/formal feel.
- **Alternative:** Clean geometric sans (e.g. Inter) — modern, app-friendly.
- **Weight:** Bold for logo; avoid thin or decorative scripts for primary lockup.

### 2.4 Deliverables

1. **Icon only**  
   - Square canvas (e.g. 1024×1024).  
   - Works at 32px (app icon), 64px (nav), 256px (splash).  
   - No wordmark.

2. **Horizontal lockup**  
   - Icon + swipeupright” (+ optional tagline).  
   - For headers, nav, light backgrounds.

3. **Stacked lockup**  
   - Icon on top, wordmark below.  
   - For splash, hero, social.

4. **Reversed**  
   - White or light icon/wordmark on pink–rose gradient or dark background.  
   - For footer, dark mode, CTAs.

5. **Clear space & minimum size**  
   - Define clear space (e.g. x = height of “P” in SwipeUpRight).  
   - Minimum width for horizontal lockup (e.g. 120px).

### 2.5 Usage Notes

- **Do:** Use on white, light gray, or soft pink/rose backgrounds; in app bars, splash, favicon, social.  
- **Avoid:** Busy backgrounds; distorting or rotating the mark; changing gradient direction or colors without approval.  
- **Export:** SVG (icon + logo), PNG (with transparency) at 1x, 2x, 3x; square PNG for app icon (e.g. 1024×1024).
